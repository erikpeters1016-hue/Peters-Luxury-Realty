How to schedule a showing:
All showings are by private appointment with one of our agents. Many of our properties require proof of funds before viewing — our team will walk you through the process. Ask the assistant to take your name and contact info, and a senior agent will reach out within 1 hour during business hours.

Financing & Proof of Funds:
For luxury transactions, sellers typically require proof of funds (POF) before accepting a showing or offer. We work with several private banks and lenders who can issue jumbo loan pre-approvals quickly and discreetly.

Buyer's Representation:
We exclusively represent buyers' interests. Our concierge service includes private showings, off-market opportunities, neighborhood orientation, and connections to top architects, designers, and contractors.

Off-Market Opportunities:
Many of LA's finest homes never hit the public market. Our network gives clients access to pocket listings and pre-market opportunities. Inquire if you'd like to be added to our private buyer's list.
